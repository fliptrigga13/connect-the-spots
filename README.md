# Connect the Dots

**By Lauren Christian Flipo** — SourceCodeMint

Connect the Dots is the name. Not a swarm. A swarm has no names. These have names, roles, limits, and one human who approves.

This repo holds the visual pieces of the Connect the Dots film, cooked in one night, November 3, 2026:

1. **Number rain** — the numbers part for the one who reads them.
2. **The knight** — the piece that moves like no other piece. I made this knight.
3. **Sign to seven** — not 7-11, not 9-11. Seven. The Seven C's: Cognitive, Corrective, Concerned, Coordinated, Compassion, Collective, Community. Article Zero: no person is disposable, and no machine takes an irreversible action on a person without a named human's bound approval.
4. **Clock spiral** — the doomsday clock is seconds to midnight. The answer is a spiral, not a circle.
5. **The watching eye** — my agents are my eyes and ears. Long live the whistleblowers.
6. **The willow** — no tombstone. Grow a willow tree out of me.

Leo, not Neo. It is me.
